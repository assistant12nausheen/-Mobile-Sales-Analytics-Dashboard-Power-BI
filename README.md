# 📱 Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze mobile phone sales performance across India — transforming raw transactional data into actionable business insights through dynamic visualizations.

---

## 📊 Overview

This dashboard provides a 360° view of mobile phone sales data, covering revenue trends, city-wise performance, brand comparisons, customer satisfaction, and payment behavior — enabling faster, data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | ₹769M |
| Total Quantity | 19K units |
| Transactions | 4K |
| Average Order Value | 40K |

## ✨ Features

- **📍 City-wise Sales Map** – Interactive geo-visualization of total sales across major Indian cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Kolkata, and more)
- **📈 Monthly Trend Analysis** – Line charts tracking total quantity sold by month to identify seasonal patterns
- **⭐ Customer Ratings Funnel** – Visual breakdown of customer satisfaction across rating levels (1–5)
- **💳 Payment Method Analysis** – Donut chart comparing UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and Cash transactions
- **🏆 Brand Performance Table** – Comparison of Apple, OnePlus, Samsung, Vivo, and Xiaomi across sales, quantity, and transactions
- **📱 Top Product Models** – Bar chart highlighting best-selling models (iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, Galaxy Note 20)
- **📅 Day-wise Sales Trends** – Analysis of sales performance across days of the week
- **🎛️ Dynamic Slicers** – Filter by Mobile Model, Payment Method, Brand, and Day Name for deep-dive analysis
- **🗓️ Month Navigation Panel** – Quick month-wise drill-down for granular reporting

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard design & development
- **DAX** – Custom measures and calculations
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **Data Modeling** – Relational data structuring for optimized performance
